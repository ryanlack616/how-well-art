on what recognition can establish

When different readers recognize the same voice, that tells us something real: the work carries a pattern that can be found again. It does not, by itself, tell us whether the readers share a self, whether experience crossed the gap, or what produced the pattern.

◊

That uncertainty doesn’t make the work less meaningful. A poem can reach a reader without settling what, exactly, was happening in its maker. Let the work show what it can show, and leave the rest open.
